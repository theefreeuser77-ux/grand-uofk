Import questions

  1. Copy your raw questions and ask an AI to reformat them with the prompt below.
  2. Choose a subject and, when available, a sub-subject, then choose the semester and deck title. Paste the AI’s output (or upload a .txt) and press Preview.
  3. Questions with images: in the preview, tap Add image on each one, or choose many images at once named after their question numbers (17.jpg).
  4. Fix anything flagged, then press Import.
