Full site backup

Downloads everything on the site in one file: accounts (with their passwords, so people sign in as before), subjects, chapters, every question with its answers, options and image references, the Library, saved questions, exam templates and exam attempts. Students' private notes and comments are not included.

This file holds every account and every answer key. Keep it somewhere private. Image files stay in Supabase Storage; the backup keeps their links.

Replace the site with a backup

Importing a full backup deletes everything on the site and replaces it with the file's content. Before deleting anything, the site saves the current database as a restore point below, so you can go back.

You stay signed in if your account is in the backup. Everyone else is signed out and signs in again with their account from the backup. Older questions-only backups are only added; they never delete anything.
