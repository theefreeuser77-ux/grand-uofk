Image processing

Upload a chapter PDF and download every figure in it as a picture, with its arrows and labels.

  1. Choose the PDF. For a whole book, write the chapter’s pages below (e.g. 120-160).
  2. Press Extract figures. A zip downloads with files named by page: p012_fig1.jpg is page 12, first figure from the top.
  3. On Import, tap Add image on each question and pick its figure.

Good to know
  • Each figure is cut out together with its labels and leader lines, but not the paragraphs around it. If a label is still cut off, run that page again with Wide.
  • Big chapters are done in parts: if the zip has a CONTINUE_FROM_PAGE.txt file, run again from the page it names.
  • Scanned books (each page is one photo) come out as whole pages.
